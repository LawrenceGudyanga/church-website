About our assembly

Rooted in Faith, Driven by Purpose

Local Assembly of the Apostolic Faith Mission of South Africa

Established in 1908, the AFM is the first and largest Pentecostal church in South Africa, with more than 1.4 million members, over 1,800 assemblies nationally, and a presence in 28 countries.

As a Pentecostal church, we believe the Spirit of God is given to every believer to live a dynamic and overcoming life, showing the character, love and power of our Lord Jesus Christ. Two inseparable characteristics define us: the baptism in the Holy Spirit and the Great Commission (Acts 1:8). That is why we see our assembly as an apostolic community and our members as apostolic agents.

“Being missional means making a Christ-centred difference where we live, work and function every day.”

Our Vision

We see a church accessible to all people, celebrating our unity and God-given diversity, and empowering our members for caring and changing communities to the glory of God.

Our Mission

Rooted in John 3:16, we are a missional church: the whole church taking the whole Gospel to the whole world, and soaking our communities in the presence and principles of the Kingdom of God.

Our Core Pillars & Values

Integrity

Being genuine, honest and sincere in all we do, as individuals and as a church body.

Relationships

Coming from a racially divided past, we treasure our unity as a gift from God and encourage genuine, edifying relationships, including spiritual mentorship.

Accountability

We believe accountability is non-negotiable and expect it at every level of leadership.

Excellence

Because we serve a God of excellence, we pursue excellence in every church activity.
